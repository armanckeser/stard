Stard

Two things to do with your GitHub stars.

Organize your stars: sort them into GitHub Lists, add tags, notes and 1 to 5 ratings, and see which starred repos are dead. Run on your machine with npx github-stars-organizer or self-host with Docker.

Play with them: every repo you starred becomes a card. Build a team and fight other teams. Type any GitHub username, no sign-in.

Also: profile cards for your README and your year in stars. Free and open source on GitHub.
